About Me

I am a PhD student in the Department of Computer Science at Colorado State University, advised by Dr. Sangmi Lee Pallickara and Dr. Shrideep Pallickara.

My research lies at the intersection of foundation models, trustworthy machine learning, and AI for Science. I am particularly interested in developing learning systems that remain reliable under distribution shifts, capture meaningful scientific structure, and operate efficiently on large-scale environmental data.

Research Interests

Trustworthy Foundation Models

Uncertainty quantification, reliability assessment, and robust prediction.

AI for Science and Environmental Modeling

Spatiotemporal learning, scientific forecasting, remote sensing, and physics-guided AI.

Efficient Representation Learning

Knowledge distillation, domain adaptation, self-supervised learning, and token reduction.
